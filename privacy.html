Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026.

This page explains how Codaxia handles information related to the Dashboard Agents landing page and Early Access purchases.

Information collected

When you purchase Dashboard Agents through Stripe, payment, billing, and receipt information is processed by Stripe. Codaxia may receive purchase details such as name, email address, amount paid, product purchased, and payment status.

How the information is used

  • To deliver the Dashboard Agents package.
  • To coordinate installation, onboarding, and follow-up support.
  • To maintain the Early Access license registry.
  • To send product updates related to the purchased license.

Payment processing

Payments are handled by Stripe. Codaxia does not store full credit card numbers on this website.

Product privacy

Dashboard Agents is local-first. The product itself does not include telemetry, online activation, or a license server. Runtime project data is stored locally on the buyer's machine.

Data sharing

Codaxia does not sell buyer data. Information may be shared only when necessary to process payment, deliver the product, provide support, or comply with legal obligations.
