The problem

AI coding assistants move fast. Their work is hard to follow.

No visible pipeline

Most AI coding workflows happen inside a terminal or chat window. You lose the overview of what is scoped, built, reviewed, and validated.

Weak handoff between roles

The assistant can act as developer, reviewer, QA, or architect, but the role changes are rarely explicit or easy to audit.

Hard to explain progress

For clients, teammates, or your future self, it is difficult to show what happened and why a task is ready.

The solution

A local dashboard for structured AI delivery.

Dashboard Agents turns invisible AI work into a visible delivery pipeline. Your assistant reads a protocol, assumes the right roles, creates tasks, logs decisions, and updates the local dashboard as it progresses.

1

Open the project in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or another compatible assistant.

2

Ask the assistant to read the agent protocol and start the mission.

3

Watch the dashboard update across agents, tasks, activity, and recap.
